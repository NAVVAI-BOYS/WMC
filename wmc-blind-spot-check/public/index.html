What this check actually is

Almost every organisation has built a defence for the inbox. Almost nobody has built one for the handset, and that is where the messages now go.

The question it answers

Can you see it, and can you stop it?

Not whether you have a tool. Whether a campaign using your name on a channel you do not monitor would ever reach you at all, and how many hours it stays live once it does.

What it is used for

Getting the conversation onto the right thing

Most of these conversations start at takedown speed. This one starts at coverage, because a fast takedown on three surfaces out of eight is not a speed problem, it is a counting problem.

What it hands you

A document you can forward

Four areas scored from your own answers, every gap written out with what it leads to if it stays open, ninety days sequenced, and a table showing which answer changed what.

What gets scored

Four areas per route, each scored 1 to 10

Every area states what a 1 and a 10 look like, so the score means something rather than being a mark out of ten. Tap any area to see both ends of its scale. Your verdict follows your weakest area, never the average, because an attacker only has to find the one thing nobody is watching.
